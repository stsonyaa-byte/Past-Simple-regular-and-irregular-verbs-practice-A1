🔥 Warm-up: How to build Past Simple

➕ PositiveSubject + past formShe played football.
She went home.
➖ NegativeSubject + didn’t + verbShe didn’t play football.
She didn’t go home.
❓ QuestionDid + subject + verb?Did she play football?
Did she go home?
Regular verbs: + -ed (ask → asked) · + -d (move → moved) · double letter (stop → stopped)
Irregular verbs: a new word (go → went). Learn them!
Remember: after didn’t and Did use the base verb (go, not went).
